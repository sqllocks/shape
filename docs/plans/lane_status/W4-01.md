## Decisions inside the package (the issue left them open)

* **Transactions are exactly Poisson and the follow-up probability is exact.** A follow-up
  (refund or reversal) races the next transaction while a transaction is pending (rate
  `lam * q / (1 - q)`, `q = refund_rate + reversal_rate`); the transaction hazard stays `lam`
  throughout. So `refund_rate + reversal_rate` must be below 1 (a `ModuleError` otherwise). The
  transaction id is in `code` (`ENTITY-N`); a follow-up carries it in `ref` and the amount in `value`.
* `telemetry_series`: `stuck_rate` is the probability that a reading repeats the previous reading
  (so stuck runs are geometric); `missing_rate` skips a slot (no event); the first reading is at
  the entity's arrival. Default `interval` is `1 day` so the conformance kit (200 entities,
  2 years, run twice) stays fast; the 1-hour case is the scale test.
* `file_arrival`: `late_delay` defaults to a quarter of the period (a fixed `2 hours` would be
  invalid for `hourly`); a duplicate is a `file_arrived` plus a `file_duplicate` a tenth of a
  period later; the rates may sum to at most 1.
* `entity_lifecycle`: `update_rate` and `delete_rate` are per entity-year hazards while alive;
  `value` is the version, `text` the state.
* `event_sequence`: `dropout` is a number or a list (one per step but the last).
