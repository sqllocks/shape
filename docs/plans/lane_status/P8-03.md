## Notes for the lead

1. **Publishing is not part of this package.** The site builds to `site/` (ignored); deploying it
   (GitHub Pages, `site_url: https://sqllocks.github.io/shape/`) needs a Pages setting and a deploy
   workflow, which P8-04 or the owner can add. Nothing here assumes it.
2. **External links are not fetched** by the link check, so CI needs no network and cannot flake
   on a third-party site. Links into this repository are checked against the checkout.
3. **P8-02 publishes to the performance page.** The page already reads whatever the harness writes
   into the committed `results.json`; once the `shape` entry is recorded, it fills in with no change
   here. Until then it says no product measurement is recorded.
4. Some existing pages mention other docs by path in backticks rather than as links; those are not
   broken and were left as written.
5. mkdocs-material prints a notice about mkdocs 2.0; T-24 pins `mkdocs<2`, so it does not apply.
6. Lint scope: `scripts/` is outside T-27; the three scripts touched here are nevertheless clean
   under `ruff check`, `ruff format --check` and `mypy --strict`. (`ruff check scripts` reports 4
   pre-existing findings in `check_requirements.py` and `check_secrets.py`, not touched here.)
