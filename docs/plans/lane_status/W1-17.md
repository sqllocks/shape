## What is in the lane

`emit`, `stream`, `scale` and the `run_to` / emit-runner entry points; `_shape_provenance.json`

guard (`src/shape/chaos/input_check.py`); docs (CHAOS, SINKS, CLI, THREAT_MODEL) and CHANGELOG.

`tests/io/test_provenance.py`.

`ipython`, `scikit-learn`, `unixodbc` and `fabric-user-data-functions` (needed to collect
`tests/demo/fabric`).
