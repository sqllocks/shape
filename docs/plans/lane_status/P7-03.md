Status: **built; awaiting lead verification.** Fixes P19.

## What was built
- `src/shape/artifact/signing.py`: Ed25519 signing over the exact `manifest.json` bytes (with a
  domain prefix), stored in the reserved member `manifest.sig` (outside `content_hashes`);
  key-pair and key-file helpers; `sign_artifact`, `verify_artifact`.
- `src/shape/artifact/io.py`: `ArtifactSignatureError`; reader accepts `manifest.sig` and takes
  `verify_key=`; writers reserve the name. `read_model`/`read_shape` take `verify_key=`.
- CLI: `shape keygen`, `shape sign`, `shape verify`, `--sign KEY` on `profile`/`capture`,
  `--verify PUBKEY` on `inspect|show|check|diff|query|plan`. A failed signature exits 1.
- `src/shape/security/crypto.py`: `public_key_from_private`, type annotations on the Ed25519 functions.
- Docs: `docs/SIGNING.md` (key handling), `docs/THREAT_MODEL.md` updated.
- Tests: `tests/artifact/test_signing.py` (P19 regression: a forged artifact with rewritten hashes
  passes the plain reader and fails verification; unsigned, stripped, wrong-key, re-signed-by-attacker, malformed, CLI).
