## Design notes

- Candidate-key search is capped at 22 searched attributes and dependency projection at 14; past that the
  engine stops with an error asking for declared keys (tested).
- Fact foreign-key columns are `NOT NULL`; loading an unknown member for missing dimension rows is left to the loader (documented).
- Snowflake moves hierarchy levels above the finest into outriggers; the date dimension stays flat.
