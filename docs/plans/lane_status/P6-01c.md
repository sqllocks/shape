- manufacturing medium is **50,520 rows**; the baseline takes 0.17 s for it. 10x means 17 ms for everything: loading the

Other tables below the floor at other Shape seeds: manufacturing small `defect` (1044), `product` (1045, 1046, 1048),
medium `quality_check` (1044, 1047), `product` (1045, 1046, 1048); iot medium `maintenance_log` (1046); marketing small
`campaign` (1049). Manufacturing and marketing are chance spread of the comparator, as in P6-01a: 1042 is among the
misses, other seeds miss other tables, and the Shape scores sit inside the baseline's own spread.

**iot medium `sensor` is different and I looked at the cause** (6 of 8 seeds miss by 0.1-0.7). The baseline's four seeds
all score 99.74-99.80, so the floor is a razor-thin 99.24. The table has two independent 8-value `weighted_enum` columns
over 12,500 rows. `FidelityComparator` scores a categorical column by a chi-square test of the candidate's counts against
the *reference's counts* (seed 42's) with a 0.05 cut-off; a rejection costs that column 4-7 points, i.e. 0.7-1.2 points
of a six-column table (`sensor_type` 95.6 at seed 1042, `unit_of_measure` 92.7 at seed 1044; the other columns score
99.4-100). Against the declared weights the chi-square statistics of both tools are those of an iid multinomial (Spindle
seeds 42-46: 3.5, 5.8, 6.3, 3.1, 5.0 and 2.6, 2.5, 5.5, 7.9, 9.6; Shape seeds: 8.4, 6.6, 3.9, 3.8, 5.5, 2.6, 13.1, 2.8
and 7.8, 6.2, 18.5, 9.8, 4.4, 8.7, 6.9, 5.8; expectation 7 for 7 degrees of freedom), so Shape's weighted enums are not
off; seed 42's own draw is close to the declared weights, so Spindle seeds 43-46 test as one chi-square against a
near-exact expectation (statistics 7.8-13.4 against the critical value 14.07) and Shape seeds, which scatter as widely, cross it
about as often as the scatter implies. A floor set from four seeds of a statistic this narrow is the effect, not a
generator difference. **Decision wanted from the owner:** accept clause (h) for these four cells as a documented chance
miss (as for P6-01a's six), or name another remedy; I did not alter the cases.
