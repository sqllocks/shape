## Decisions and ambiguities (lead: please confirm or correct)

1. **Opt-in generation** (your decision): the pass is `--mixed-copula` (CLI of `shape generate --from` and
   `shape plan`) and `mixed_copula=True` (`shape.generate`, `fit_schema`). Off: the schema has no `copula_mixed`
   block and the generated tables are equal to those of a profile without `joint.copula` (test). The plan lists
   `joint.copula` as `not_modelled` (with the flag as the way in) when the profile has it and the flag is off.
2. **Cohorts: two guards beyond the 0.25 silhouette.** The issue's known answers cannot both hold with the
   silhouette alone: an unclustered normal sample has a best silhouette of about 0.56 in one dimension, 0.33 in
   two and 0.23 in three (measured; scipy-free k-means, n = 1,500), so a single Gaussian of one or two columns
   would give cohorts. The entry is therefore stored only when the best silhouette is at least 0.25 (as
   specified) **and** beats the best silhouette of a single Gaussian with the same covariance by 0.10, **and**
   the partition is stable (the adjusted Rand index between the partition and the ones fitted on random halves of
   the sample is at least 0.90). "Stored only when the best silhouette is at least 0.25" stays true. The third
   guard was added after a false-positive sweep of `cohort_shift` on random mixed tables (5 of 120 before, 0 of
   320 after): k-means on a skewed single-mode cloud splits it differently on every sample.
3. **The latent matrix is bridged, not the raw normal-score correlation.** "Estimated from normal scores
   (mid-ranks for categories), made positive definite" is followed, with one correction: the normal scores of a
   three-level category correlate about 0.8 times as strongly as the latent normal behind them, and reordering
   by the rank of a correlated latent normal attenuates a second time. Used as is, a category-to-number ratio of
   0.62 came back as 0.54 and a Cramer's V of 0.53 as 0.30 (measured), outside the 0.05 of the acceptance. Each
   pair's latent correlation is therefore the one whose generated normal-score correlation equals the profile's
   (Mehler expansion in Hermite polynomials, bisection; `multivariate._latent_correlation`). With it all six
   test data sets stay within 0.02.
4. **Conditional-table columns move with their source instead of being left alone.** The issue says columns
   held by a conditional table are left alone. A profile that has both a category-to-number link and a
   category-to-category link has a conditional table for the second (Cramer's V of 0.25 or more, 30 levels or
   fewer), which would hold the category and lose its link to the number. A column that `conditional_table`
   columns are drawn from is reordered **together with them**: the rows of the group are permuted as one, so the
   table still holds (its Cramer's V is unchanged) and every marginal is exact. Hierarchy columns, keys, primary
   keys and columns another column's generator or another table reads (derived, formula, lookup, computed,
   foreign key) are left alone, as specified. If you want the literal rule, `copula_mixed._followers` returning
   `{}` and `_held` holding `conditional_table` columns restores it; the profile-only data sets then keep both
   links (tests marked "no-conditional-table").
5. **The mixed copula replaces the numeric copula for the columns it orders.** A column the mixed pass orders is
   dropped from the numeric copula's pairs (the mixed matrix holds the same correlations as normal scores);
   other pairs are untouched.
6. **"`shape show` and the `shape profile` text output".** `shape show` (alias of `inspect`) prints the whole
   profile as JSON, so it prints the new entries; `shape profile` prints `{written, id}` and has `--html` and
   `--json` (the summary, whose key set `tests/demo/core/test_profile.py` pins, so unchanged). I added a "Joint
   structure" card of one line per entry to the `--html` report (`describe_joint`). A text table or flag would be a
   new command surface; say so if wanted.
7. **Choices the issue leaves open.** Two-column determinants also need lift 0.3 over the dependent's baseline, at
   least five repeated groups (as single-column ones do) and half the rows in repeated groups (a pair whose
   combinations mostly occur once determines anything). MCD uses numeric columns with at least 10 distinct values
   (fewer make the h-subset covariance singular) and drops collinear columns, uses complete rows, and applies one
   reweighting step after the raw estimate (as the reference implementation does; without it the small-sample
   false-alarm rate of the 0.999 cut was several times larger). `contributions` is the squared standardised
   residual of the column given the others, normalised per row (non-negative, sums to 1). PCA fills a missing
   value with the column mean. The copula leaves out key-like names, unique columns and categoricals of more than
   200 levels. Cohort summaries carry `mean` / `std` and `mode` / `share`.
8. **Noise guards** (documented in `docs/DRIFT.md`): `multivariate_outlier_rate_change` needs the rise to beat
   six binomial standard errors (a robust fit moves with the sample: the rate of a skewed table varies up to 1.6
   times its binomial spread, measured; three standard errors failed `test_two_samples_of_one_distribution_do_not_drift`);
   `structure_change`'s angle test needs the angle to beat three times the asymptotic sampling angle of the
   spectrum with the gaps shrunk by three eigenvalue standard errors (sample gaps are wider than true ones);
   `cohort_shift` needs three standard errors summed over the cohorts. A false-positive sweep over 320 random
   mixed tables (two sizes each) reports none of the three kinds.
9. **Found and fixed: `shape generate --from` wrote nothing for a table the copula reorders.** `Engine._generate`
   never handed a table in `copula` to the writer (`release()` kept every copula table in `later` for good), so
   `-f csv -o DIR` printed "Wrote 1 csv files" and left the directory empty for any profile with a numeric
   correlation (checked on the base tree). One line in `engine.py::release`; regression test
   `test_a_table_the_copula_changes_is_written_once_it_is_final`. Not in Appendix A: the lead may want a row.
10. `fit_schema` lists `pca`, `cohorts` and `multivariate_outliers` as `not_modelled` in the plan (generation
    per cohort is out of scope).
