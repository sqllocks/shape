## Decisions to review (the issue left them open)

- **Skew thresholds other than PSI.** The issue fixes only PSI (flagged at 0.2 or more). I chose
  `null_rate` 0.05 (absolute difference), `unseen_category_share` 0.01, `out_of_range_share` 0.01,
  all strict "more than" and all settable; `label_rate_diff` has no default (the label is reported,
  flagged only when set, since serving data often has no labels). Documented as screening defaults.
- **`shape.yml` source.** W1-04 has not landed here. I read `sources.NAME.thresholds` (the layout in
  `lane/W1-04`'s `docs/PROJECT.md`) with `--project FILE|DIR` and `--source NAME`, keeping only the
  skew keys and ignoring the drift ones; flags override. When W1-04 lands, `_project_thresholds` in
  `cli/skew.py` is the only place that should switch to its loader.
- **Small slices.** Two or more are pooled as `(small slices)`; a single one is left out (only its
  count recorded) because a pool of one is that slice shown alone. In skew a slice needs the minimum
  on both sides. The pooled slice is excluded from the disparity ratio.
- **Label positive value.** boolean: true; two numbers: the larger; two words only if they are a
  known true/false pair; else exit 2 asking to recode (no `--positive` flag, not on the list).
- **Profiles in skew.** A profile holds no values: schema and null-rate skew only; PSI, unseen
  and out-of-range are `null` with a note; `--slice-by` with a profile exits 2.
- **Skew slice labels.** Classified slice columns are numbered; skew has no `--show-classified`
  (not on the list).
- **Reference profile.** Needs category shares (a column of few distinct values) and one slice column.
- **`missing_from_data`.** Reference values absent from the data, up to 20, by reference share.
