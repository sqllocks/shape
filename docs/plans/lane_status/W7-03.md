## Decisions and ambiguities (lead: please confirm or correct)

1. **Display.** As in W3-07, `shape profile` prints JSON, not a text table; `shape show`/`cat`
   print every field (tested), and the `--html` report cards state `mixture: k=...` and
   `seasonality: period ...` (tested). No new text command was added.
2. **Mixture sample cap is 4,000 values** (the issue allows up to 100,000): EM is run four times per
   numeric column on every profile and costs about 0.2 s per column at 4,000 values on this machine
   (200 iterations are reached for k = 3 and 4 on data with no extra component). Tolerance 1e-6 mean
   log-likelihood per value, 200 iterations, variance floor 0.1% of the column's sd.
3. **Seasonality details the issue leaves open** (all in `docs/PROFILING_NOTES.md`): hourly series
   only has candidate 24; 12 and 52 are regroupings of the daily series (calendar months; runs of 7
   days); empty periods are interpolated and a series with more than half of its periods empty is
   `applicable: false`; applicable entries also carry `applicable: true`; every numeric column of a
   table without a time column gets `applicable: false, reason: "no date or timestamp column"`
   (literal reading of the issue); a time zone is ignored; workbook sheets are not analysed.
4. **Noise guards on the diff kinds** (documented in `docs/DRIFT.md`), as W3-07 did for its kinds:
   `mixture_change` needs the KS distance between the two fitted mixtures to beat the critical value
   (alpha = 0.001) because the `k` of skewed data flips between samples of one distribution;
   `seasonality_change`: a period change counts only when both sides are seasonal, a flag flip needs
   a strength move of 0.1, and a side that is not applicable gives nothing to compare. Thresholds
   `mixture_weight` = 0.1 and `seasonality_strength` = 0.2 are in `DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS`.
5. **Result shape for strength.** `violations` holds the rules that fail the check and `warnings`
   the broken ones that do not; `strength` and `warnings` appear only when the contract declares a
   `strength` somewhere (otherwise the output is byte-identical to before, as the issue requires).
   `--strict` moves everything into `violations`.
6. `fd`/`implies`/`reference_pair` entries and `row_count` take a word only; the object form is for
   a column's rules. The error text names the column and the rule (or the fd/implies/reference pair).
7. `mixture` and `seasonality` are in `univariate.FIELDS`, so generation lists them as "not
   modelled" (out of scope), and `shape diff` between a pre-change profile and a new one lists them
   as changed fields in the registry diff (the same as W3-07's).
8. The new `docs/CONTRACTS.md` is the contract documentation (there was no contract page);
   README and CLI.md point to the same behaviour.
