   unpinned or unhashed entry, or a declared dependency that is missing or out of range. CI job
   `offline-lock` generates, checks and uploads them. Documented in `docs/INSTALL.md`. Tests:
   `tests/release/test_offline_lock.py`. Generation was run for real here: all 17 sets resolved
   and passed `check`.

   `build` job runs it with `--wheel`; it is also in `make check`. Tests:
   `tests/release/test_shipped_data.py`. Run against a locally built wheel: passes.
