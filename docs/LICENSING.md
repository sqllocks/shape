Generated data belongs to you. Shape claims no rights in it. Your own profiles stay yours.
This statement does not remove obligations attached to reference data incorporated into an
output or rights in the source rows. Review the notices and your source-data permissions.
A safe capture is data minimisation, not anonymisation.
