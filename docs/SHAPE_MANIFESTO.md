# Shape Manifesto

Status: experimental.


1. Shape represents behavior, not records.
2. Shape never claims to recover original source records.
3. Observed facts and inference are different things.
4. Uncertainty is explicit.
5. Approximation is acceptable; hidden approximation is not.
6. Privacy loss and fidelity loss are explicit.
7. Capture is streaming/bounded-memory by default.
8. Statistics are mergeable wherever practical.
9. Generated data must be measurable against the Shape that produced it.
10. Domain knowledge belongs in Packs, not core.
11. Expectations belong in Policy, not Shape.
12. Historical observations belong in History, not the current Shape.
13. AI may explain and author intent; the statistical engine does not depend on AI.
14. Reproducibility is a feature.
15. Performance is a feature and must be measured.
16. A Shape declares its capabilities rather than pretending to contain information it did not capture.
17. Share the Shape, not the data — but never equate that slogan with an unproven privacy guarantee.
18. Capture once. Compile for purpose.
19. Store the minimum information necessary to reproduce the maximum useful behavior.

20. A Shape can itself be sensitive or classified.
21. Synthetic does not mean declassified.
22. No operation silently lowers handling requirements.
23. Core Shape works offline; external transmission is optional and policy-controlled.
24. Security capability is not the same thing as certification or accreditation.
